## Summary

JPM maintains its pro-cyclical framework despite recent market volatility, with FX remaining a "sideshow" compared to broader deleveraging in crypto, equities and metals. The key development is AUD/USD breaking above high 60s on the back of the RBA's hawkish hike, creating positive policy spreads versus the US for the first time since 2018. This move is worth 4-5 big figures on rate differentials alone, with another hike expected in May pushing targets to the low 70s mid-year.

A central theme emerging is euro/APAC rotation, where EMEA currencies are facing central bank pushback on strength while APAC benefits from renewed cyclical momentum. The Riksbank's concern over krona strength exemplifies this, though JPM views it as a fade given the underlying growth recovery. Similar dynamics are developing in EMFX, particularly in CEE where currencies like PLN and CZK face dovish risks from disinflationary surprises.

Sterling faces renewed political risk premium around fiscal concerns, but JPM argues the improving activity backdrop breaks last year's "doom loop" dynamic. The BoE's dovish surprise is viewed as temporary given pricing is already near terminal rate estimates. Meanwhile, Japan's weekend election presents binary risks for USD/JPY, though the medium-term 164 target remains unchanged regardless of outcome.

US data continues showing the growth/labor market dichotomy, with Warsh's Fed nomination not materially changing the strategic bearish USD view. The team expects limited easing capacity given committee dynamics, with balance sheet changes likely too gradual to significantly impact FX markets.
