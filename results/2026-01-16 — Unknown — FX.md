## Summary

The episode focuses on two key political risks: Fed independence concerns following the DOJ subpoena of Chair Powell, and Japan's snap election announcement. Despite the dramatic nature of Powell's video response, USD reaction was muted due to institutional pushback, Republican opposition (notably Senator Tillis), and market expectations that Powell may stay as governor post-chair term. Patrick argues the USD deserves more risk premium given ongoing political uncertainty, with Lisa Cook's January 21st hearings representing the biggest Fed independence risk ahead.

Japan's February snap election has reignited the "Takaichi trade" (long JGB/short JPY), with market focus on whether the LDP can secure 233 seats for a majority. Initial reactions are expected to be binary: LDP majority accelerates the trade, while failure triggers unwinding. However, Junior emphasizes that medium-term JPY bearishness remains intact regardless of outcome, as fiscal discipline won't materially change and BoJ won't accelerate hikes.

The CNY has seen dramatic strengthening to 696 from 700+, driven by PBOC fixing guidance and record corporate dollar selling ahead of Lunar New Year. The team views this as politically motivated, particularly toward Europe given trade surplus concerns, making EUR/CNH a distinct policy cross that should continue declining even if EUR strengthens elsewhere.
