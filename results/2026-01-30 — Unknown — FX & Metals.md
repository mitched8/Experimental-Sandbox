## Summary

The team maintains their strategic bearish USD view despite the recent acceleration in moves, emphasizing the pro-cyclical macro backdrop remains intact. The catalyst for recent dollar weakness was reported Fed rate checks on USD/JPY, though Bessent denied that intervention WAS CONDUCTED (in the past), not that there are plans (future). The summary mischaracterizes the timing of his denial.. Metals have seen explosive moves with gold reaching 5,500 before pulling back to 5,000, driven by diversification flows into real assets amid debt sustainability concerns. Greg Ferrer maintains structural bullish gold views supported by central bank purchases, while silver appears vulnerable to correction from speculative overextension.

The antipodeans continue to benefit from the cyclical backdrop, with AUD fair value models suggesting slight additional upside driven primarily by commodity exposure, particularly copper. New Zealand has seen a significant upgrade with RBNZ hiking expectations brought forward to Q3 2026 as business confidence translates into actual recovery metrics. The team now forecasts 100bp of total tightening versus 50bp previously.

Asia FX shows increasing policy coordination, with Korea's NPS reducing FX hedging by $20bn and Malaysia demonstrating policy permissiveness since Trump's October visit. CNY continues its controlled decline with PBOC fixings accelerating to 6.9678, maintaining the "moderately strong RMB" guidance. The options market shows unusual dynamics with dollar-down/vol-up correlation breaking historical patterns, particularly in EUR/USD where skews briefly touched Liberation Day highs, suggesting potential for explosive moves higher despite macro team's measured bullishness.
