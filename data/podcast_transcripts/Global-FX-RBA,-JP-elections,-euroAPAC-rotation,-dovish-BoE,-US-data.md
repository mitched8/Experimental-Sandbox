# Global FX: RBA, JP elections, euro/APAC rotation, dovish BoE, US data

> **Published:** Fri, 06 Feb 2026 13:55:16 -0500
> **Author:** J.P. Morgan Global Research

## Summary

This JP Morgan FX strategy podcast covers global currency developments with analysts from Sydney to New York. Host Meera Chandan notes that while broader markets experienced volatility and deleveraging, FX remained relatively sidelined compared to moves in crypto, equities, and metals. The team maintains their pro-cyclical view supporting growth currencies and bearish dollar outlook despite recent US data strength.

The discussion begins with APAC currencies, particularly the Australian dollar's strong performance following an RBA rate hike that created positive policy spreads versus the US for the first time since 2018. Ben Jarman highlights this as crystallizing the bullish Aussie case, with targets raised to the lower 70s. For Japan, Junior discusses upcoming elections and GPIF allocation reports, maintaining a medium-term bearish yen view with targets at 164 regardless of election outcomes.

European currencies feature prominently, with James defending Sterling despite BoE dovishness and political uncertainties, arguing the UK is breaking out of last year's fiscal doom loop due to improving activity. The Riksbank's pushback on Swedish krona strength is viewed as fadeable given underlying bullish fundamentals.

The podcast explores a key theme of regional rotation from Eurozone to APAC currencies, with Aneshka noting extreme valuations in EMEA EM versus Asia. US discussion centers on mixed data showing growth resilience but labor market weakness, with Patrick arguing Kevin Warsh's Fed nomination doesn't change their strategic dollar bearish view given limited committee dynamics and already-discounted market expectations.
